## About the PI and PolyU RCEV
Please check [here](README.md) to learn more about PI Dr Mingyuan JIANG.

[PolyU Research Centre for Electric Vehicles (RCEV)](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/rcev/) is a new university-level research centre which was launched on 26 Feb 2024. The Director of the Research Centre is **[Prof. Ching-Chuen Chan, GBS](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/eee/people/academic-staff-and-teaching-staff/prof-chan-ching-chuen/)**, Distinguished Chair Professor of Electric Vehicles and Smart Energy in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), who is **the first Academician of CAE (中国工程院院士) in Hong Kong**, and also a renowned expert in the field and widely recognised as the “Father of Asian electric vehicles”. The Co-Director is **[Prof. Kwok-Tong Chau](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/eee/people/academic-staff-and-teaching-staff/prof-chau-kwok-tong/)**, Chair Professor of Electrical Energy Engineering in EEE. RCEV aims to address the technical challenges of modern electric vehicles (EVs) and provide significant technological solutions. By integrating EV technology with energy, information, humanity, and policy, the Centre follows the philosophy of integrating the human, cyber, and physical worlds. One of the Centre's primary goals is to transfer the developed technologies to industries, particularly within the Greater Bay Area, enabling them to capitalise on the abundant opportunities presented by EVs globally. This technology transfer will also play a vital role in supporting the realisation of carbon neutrality in various countries and cities, including Hong Kong.

# PDF Applications
## Brief Description
Applications are invited for a research position in an industry-collaborative project *"Axial-Flux Dual-Rotor Humanoid Robot Leg Joint Motor" (ITS/022/26FX)*, focusing on high-performance electric machines for humanoid robot joints. The project aims to develop an innovative axial-flux dual-rotor motor featuring a shared stator and integrated winding system, enabling independently controllable mechanical outputs.

## Research Responsibilities
- Design and optimization of axial-flux dual-rotor and multi-port electric machines.
- Development of motor drive and control strategies.
- Prototype development, experimental testing, and performance evaluation.
- Preparation of technical reports, publications, and project deliverables.

## Requirements
- **PhD degree** in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a related STEM discipline. Background in electric machines, electromagnetic analysis, or motor drives.
- **University requirements** according to [RTH-ITF Application Guide](https://www.itf.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_90/RTH-ITF_Guide_en_Mar_2025.pdf) (local = Hong Kong):
  - **For local universities**: No additional Top 100 university ranking requirement.
  - **For non-local universities**: The degree-awarding university should be ranked among the **Top 100 in a relevant STEM subject area** under at least one of the recognized ranking systems:
    - QS World University Rankings by Subject
    - Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings by Subject
    - Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU / ShanghaiRanking)
    - U.S. News Best Global Universities Rankings by Subject
  - Important Notes:
    - The **Top 100 requirement refers to eligible STEM subject rankings**, rather than necessarily the overall university ranking. Meeting the university ranking requirement does not automatically guarantee RTH-ITF eligibility.
    - If the university does not meet the Top 100 requirement, alternative academic qualification assessments may be submitted for ITC's consideration, subject to approval.
    - Final eligibility is subject to verification and approval by ITC.
- Experience in **axial-flux machines, multi-rotor motors, robotic actuators, or experimental motor testing** would be an advantage.
- Experience with Ansys Maxwell, JMAG, MATLAB/Simulink, or equivalent tools.
- Good analytical, communication, and independent research skills.

## Remuneration
 The maximum monthly salary allowance **HKD 35,000** + additional monthly living allowance of **HKD 10,000** for a maximum of **36 months**.
